About me

I am a condensed matter theorist interested in strongly correlated systems. My research examines how electron interactions, multiple orbitals, and spin-orbit coupling give rise to unconventional magnetic and quantum phases.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Oak Ridge National Laboratory. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Tennessee in 2020 under the supervision of Prof. Elbio Dagotto.
